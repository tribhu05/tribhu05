## 🎯 About Me

I'm a second-year **B.Tech CSE (AI/ML)** student at **VIT Bhopal**, currently building intelligent systems that go from research paper to actually working. My interests span **computer vision, deep learning, and full-stack development**, with a growing focus on deploying models people can actually use.

Outside of coursework, I run **Nexora**, a YouTube channel where I write and animate documentary-style explainer videos entirely in a hand-drawn doodle style, covering everything from ancient history to physics.
